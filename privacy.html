Who we are

Tshepe Game Farm ("Tshepe", "we", "us") is a private game farm approximately 15 km outside Bela-Bela, Waterberg, Limpopo, South Africa. We are the responsible party for the personal information described in this policy, as defined in the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

For any privacy question or request, contact Marius Struwig, WhatsApp 082 610 7583.

What this policy covers

This policy explains how we handle personal information when you use this website, enquire about a stay and, if you book, when you stay with us. It applies to guests, people enquiring on behalf of a group, and visitors to this website.

Information we collect

Enquiries. The "Plan your stay" planner runs in your browser. It does not send your details to us or store them on a server. When you choose "Open in WhatsApp", the message is placed in WhatsApp on your device, and nothing reaches us until you press send. A sent enquiry can include your name, contact number, email address (optional), dates, number of guests, activities of interest and any notes you add, such as the ages of children joining activities.

Bookings and stays. If you book, we may ask for the information needed to confirm and manage your stay, such as guest names and contact details, and, for shooting or hunting activities, the details that South African law requires.

Website technical data. Our hosting provider processes standard technical information, such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to deliver the website and protect it from abuse. We do not use analytics or advertising tools on this website.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a quote, at your request.
  • To confirm and manage your booking and stay, as part of our agreement with you.
  • To keep guests safe and meet legal requirements, for example for firearm handling and hunting.
  • To keep this website secure and working, which is in our legitimate interest.

We do not send marketing messages unless you have asked to receive them, and you can opt out at any time.

Is providing information required?

Providing information is voluntary. We need at least your name and a contact number to reply to an enquiry, and some details are required by law for shooting and hunting activities. If you choose not to provide them, we may not be able to reply, confirm a booking or arrange those activities.

Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information. We share it only where needed:

  • WhatsApp (Meta), when you send us a message through WhatsApp.
  • Netlify, our website hosting provider, for the technical data described above.
  • People involved in your stay, such as a Professional Hunter or a cook you have asked for, only as far as they need it.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

Information sent outside South Africa

WhatsApp and Netlify may process information outside South Africa. Where this happens, we rely on the grounds that section 72 of POPIA allows, including the providers' binding data protection terms and, where applicable, your consent when you choose to contact us through WhatsApp.

How long we keep it

We keep personal information only for as long as we need it for the purpose it was collected, or for as long as the law requires us to keep records. Enquiries that do not lead to a booking are deleted once they are no longer needed.

How we protect it

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information. This website is served only over a secure HTTPS connection with modern security headers, it has no user accounts or passwords, and the planner does not transmit or store your details on a server. Access to booking information is limited to the people who need it.

Cookies and browser storage

This website does not use advertising, analytics or other tracking cookies, and our fonts are hosted on this website, so no third-party requests are made when you browse. We use your browser's local storage only as follows:

Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask us to:

  • confirm whether we hold information about you and give you access to it;
  • correct or update it;
  • delete it where we no longer have a lawful reason to keep it;
  • stop using it, or object to how we use it, including for direct marketing; and
  • withdraw any consent you have given.

To make a request, contact Marius Struwig, WhatsApp 082 610 7583. We may need to confirm your identity before we act on a request.

Children

We collect information about children, such as their ages, only from a parent or guardian, and only to plan suitable, supervised activities during a family stay.

Complaints

If you are concerned about how we handle your personal information, please contact us first so we can try to resolve it. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator:

  • Website: inforegulator.org.za
  • Complaints: POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za
  • General enquiries: enquiries@inforegulator.org.za
  • Address: Woodmead North Office Park, 54 Maxwell Drive, Woodmead, Johannesburg, 2191

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

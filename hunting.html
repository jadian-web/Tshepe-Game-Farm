The hunting experience

The hunt is only part of the experience.

The bushveld. The hunt. The stories that follow. Responsible hunting guided by a Professional Hunter (PH) on 800 hectares of private bushveld.

More than a hunt

Learning, adventure and shared memories.

At Tshepe, hunting is more than the hunt. Set on 800 hectares of private bushveld, it is an experience of learning, adventure and shared memories.

Ideal for families, first-time hunters, small local groups and corporate outings, Tshepe offers supervised firearm safety guidance and opportunities to introduce children to nature and responsible hunting.

Enquire about South African biltong hunting and international hunting arrangements. Marius will confirm the available options, current species, rates and requirements.

Discuss your hunting requirements with Marius, who will confirm the available options and arrangements for your group.

How a hunt works

From the range to the fire.

Every hunt follows the farm's confirmed safety and hunting conditions, with a Professional Hunter guiding your group.

  1. Before the hunt

    Start at the range

    Confirm dates, available species, rates and Professional Hunter arrangements with us, then begin at the air-conditioned underground shooting range with safety guidance.

  2. In the veld

    Out with your Professional Hunter

    Head into the veld with a Professional Hunter on specially equipped hunting quad bikes or the game vehicle, under the farm's safety and hunting conditions.

  3. Beyond the hunt

    Stories by the fire

    Return to the lodge for shared meals and time around the boma, with plenty for non-hunting guests to enjoy. A cook is available on request.

By prior arrangement
  • Guided hunting outings with a PH and supervised firearm safety guidance
  • Biltong and international hunting enquiries options confirmed by Marius
  • Underground shooting range air-conditioned, with distances up to 100 m
  • Specially equipped hunting quad bikes or game vehicles
  • Butchery / slaughter room for meat processing, scope confirmed when booking
  • Outdoor target shooting and clay pigeon shooting
Before you bookAvailable species, rates, permits, guiding, equipment and ammunition are confirmed directly with Tshepe. Firearm handling and hunting follow safety rules and supervision, with no firearm handling or shooting after consuming alcohol.
Who it's for

Hunting experiences for different guests.

First-time hunters
A learning experience with introductory firearm safety guidance and supervised time in the veld with a PH.
Families and children
Responsible hunting as a family learning experience. Children take part only when approved by the host, under the required adult and professional supervision.
Friends and local hunting groups
Private group time, a Professional Hunter, the lodge, meals together and the stories that follow.
Corporate outings
Hunting or supervised shooting tailored and confirmed for your team, with shared experiences beyond the veld.
Biltong hunters
South African hunters can enquire about biltong hunting. Marius will confirm current options, species and rates.
International hunters
Visiting hunters can enquire about international hunting arrangements, requirements and accommodation.
Beyond the hunt

More than time in the veld.

The experience continues after time in the veld. Share stories around the fire, explore the farm and enjoy a comfortable stay with family or fellow hunters. There is plenty to enjoy between guided hunting activities, with a cook available on request and on-site facilities that make a group visit easier.

  • Game drives
  • Quad biking
  • Underground shooting range
  • Outdoor target shooting
  • Clay pigeon shooting
  • Fishing at the small dam
  • Clubhouse with pool table
  • Pool and boma
  • Sundowners and picnics
  • Walks and mountain biking

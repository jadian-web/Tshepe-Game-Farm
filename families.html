Families & Leisure

Family time, the bushveld way.

Leave busy schedules behind and enjoy the Waterberg together. Families with children are welcome.

A little adventure

A lot of time together.

Bring your family together for a bushveld break approximately 15 km outside Bela-Bela. Explore the Waterberg on a game drive, look out for wildlife, arrange a bush picnic or sundowner, or simply relax at the swimming pool.

Share relaxed mornings, family meals and evenings around the boma, with the flexibility to spend time together without following a fixed programme.

Ways to spend your stay

Plan it around your family.

Every family is different. Mix and match these, and we'll help you plan activities to suit the ages and interests of your group.

Nature and discovery
Game drives, wildlife viewing, family-friendly nature experiences, birdlife and time appreciating the bushveld.
Outdoor adventure
Walks, mountain biking, quad biking and fishing at the small dam, where suitable for your group.
Quality time together
The swimming pool, communal meals, indoor and outdoor braais, boma evenings, and the clubhouse with its pool table.
A special shared moment
Bush sundowners, bush picnics, or a cook on request, planned and priced with your host in advance.
Families with hunters
Non-hunting family members can enjoy the lodge and suitable activities while hunting plans are arranged separately with the PH and host.
Explore together

Out in the veld

Ask about game drives, wildlife viewing and family-friendly nature experiences, or plan walks, mountain biking, quad biking and fishing at the small dam where suitable. Availability, ages, supervision and safety requirements are confirmed for each activity.

Unwind together

Back at the lodge

Spend the afternoon beside the swimming pool, prepare a family meal in the communal kitchen, gather at the braai or relax under the stars around the boma. A cook can also be requested in advance.

Accommodation for your family

Room for everyone, grandparents included.

Seven bedrooms accommodate 14 guests sharing, with an optional open loft for two additional guests. Confirm your preferred room and bed arrangements when booking.

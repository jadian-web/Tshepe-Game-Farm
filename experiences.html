Experiences

A little adventure. A lot of bushveld.

There is more than one way to enjoy Tshepe. Set your own pace, or plan active days around your group.

Activities

Adventure in nature.

Discover outdoor experiences on 800 hectares of private bushveld, where open spaces, wildlife and natural beauty create a backdrop for adventure, relaxation and connection.

Wildlife

Game drives

Explore the farm and discover its wildlife and Waterberg scenery on a drive arranged for your group.

Adventure

Quad biking

Add a little adventure on approved farm routes, with safety requirements confirmed in advance.

Supervised

Indoor shooting range

Supervised range sessions in a purpose-built indoor facility, also the first step of every hunt.

Supervised

Target & clay pigeon shooting

Outdoor target shooting and clay pigeon shooting, with supervision and safety rules.

Water

Fishing at the small dam

A relaxed change of pace beside the water, subject to arrangements and conditions.

Outdoors

Walks & mountain biking

Take in the open spaces at a slower pace on the farm's permitted routes.

Social

Sundowners & bush picnics

A memorable pause in the bushveld, arranged around your group.

Families

Nature experiences

Introduce children to the sights and sounds of the Waterberg, planned around age and supervision.

On site

Pool, boma & clubhouse

Between adventures: swim, gather around the fire or challenge someone to a game of pool.

Please noteActivities are subject to prior arrangement, availability, weather and applicable safety conditions. Some may carry additional charges or need special equipment. We confirm the exact arrangements for each group.

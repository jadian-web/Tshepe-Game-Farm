Groups, meetings & celebrations

Your gathering. Your way.

Small teams. Private gatherings. Shared experiences. Private, personal and memorable.

Meet. Connect. Celebrate.

Bring people together.

From small team retreats and strategy sessions to birthdays and family gatherings, our private bushveld setting creates room to connect. Families with children are welcome, with suitable experiences by arrangement.

  • Family gatheringsBirthdays, reunions, anniversaries and meaningful time across generations.
  • Friends' getawaysPrivate weekends, outdoor activities, shared meals and evenings around the fire.
  • Small team retreatsStrategy sessions, meetings, planning days and informal team connection.
  • Private celebrationsIntimate gatherings centred on your overnight group and the shared experience.
  • Corporate hunting outingsGuided hunting and team experiences with comfortable private lodge accommodation.

Not just a venue. A weekend together.

Whether you are here to think together, celebrate a milestone or simply reconnect, Tshepe offers the privacy and flexibility to make the occasion your own.

For teams

Beyond the boardroom.

Fresh surroundings · New perspectives · Stronger connections

Step away from the usual office setting and give your team space for focused conversations and shared experiences. Tshepe welcomes small team getaways, business meetings, strategy sessions and intimate corporate retreats. A meeting room and outdoor activities can be arranged to suit the group.

Prepare meals together in the communal kitchen and spacious dining area, or ask about a cook on request so the whole team can focus on the time together.

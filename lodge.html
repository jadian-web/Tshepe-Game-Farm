The Lodge

Space to stay together.

Luxurious, well-appointed accommodation for small groups and families. Your private base in the bushveld, for up to 16 guests.

Bedrooms

Your luxury escape in the bush.

Seven named, air-conditioned bedrooms and an open loft create a relaxing retreat after time exploring the bushveld.

Seven bedrooms and an open loft.

Choose from seven named bedrooms: Buffalo with a king-size bed, and Springbok, Sable, Zebra, Wildebeest, Kudu and Giraffe with queen-size beds. The open loft next to Rooms 2 and 3 has two double beds. Springbok is upstairs, Zebra is a smaller room with a shower-only en-suite, and Wildebeest, Kudu and Giraffe are freestanding units. Sable has a separate bathroom; the other bedrooms have en-suite bathrooms. Bed configurations are fixed as listed.

The lodge accommodates up to 16 overnight guests. Tell us who is travelling and we'll help you plan who sleeps where.

Shared living

Spaces for togetherness.

Tshepe's communal spaces make it easy to gather, share meals and relax together. Cook in the fully equipped kitchen, spend afternoons beside the pool, and evenings around the boma. Gather. Share. Unwind.

Facilities

Everything for an easy group stay.

Tshepe is a self-catering lodge. A cook is available on request, and daily cleaning and supplied firewood make group stays easier.

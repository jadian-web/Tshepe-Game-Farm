Good to know
Questions guests ask.

    

        
How many guests can Tshepe accommodate?
The seven bedrooms accommodate up to 14 guests sharing. An optional open loft can accommodate two additional guests, bringing the maximum to 16 overnight guests.

        
Is the lodge self-catering?
Yes. Guests have access to a fully equipped communal self-catering kitchen and generous dining and gathering spaces. A cook can be arranged on request.

        
Is Tshepe suitable for families with children?
Yes. Families with children are welcome. The lodge has shared living spaces and a swimming pool, and suitable nature and outdoor activities can be arranged in advance. Participation is subject to age, supervision and safety requirements.

        
What can non-hunting family members do?
Guests who are not hunting can enjoy the lodge, pool, boma and clubhouse, and ask about suitable game drives, fishing, nature activities, bush picnics and sundowners by prior arrangement.

        
Are activities available during a stay?
Guests can enquire about game drives, wildlife viewing, quad biking, walks, mountain biking, fishing, shooting activities, sundowners and bush picnics. All organised activities require advance arrangement.

        
Can we plan a team meeting or private gathering?
Yes. Tshepe welcomes small team retreats, strategy sessions, family gatherings and private celebrations. Contact us to discuss meeting arrangements and overnight accommodation.

        
Does Tshepe offer guided hunting?
Yes. PH-guided hunting is available by prior enquiry. Ask about current species availability, rates, permits and equipment requirements.

        
Where is Tshepe Game Farm?
Approximately 15 km outside Bela-Bela in the Waterberg, Limpopo. Detailed directions are shared when your booking is confirmed.

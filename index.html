Welcome to Tshepe

A place to gather. Room to unwind.

A good bushveld getaway is about more than where you sleep. It is about the conversations that run late, the mornings that start slowly and the stories you take home.

On 800 hectares of private bushveld approximately 15 km outside Bela-Bela, Tshepe Game Farm gives families, friends and small groups space to enjoy all of it. Luxurious, well-appointed accommodation sits alongside welcoming communal spaces, a swimming pool, braai facilities and an outdoor boma.

Explore the farm on game drives and outdoor adventures, plan a focused team retreat or arrange a guided hunting weekend. However you spend your time, Tshepe is yours to share.

Your Tshepe experience

Who's coming along?

Plan a bushveld stay around your group. Choose an option below to explore the possibilities.

Family time, the bushveld way.

A little adventure and a lot of time together. Share relaxed mornings, family meals and evenings around the boma, with outdoor experiences planned around the ages and interests of your group. Families with children are welcome.

  • Game drives and family-friendly nature experiences
  • Fishing at the small dam, walks and mountain biking
  • Afternoons at the pool, evenings at the boma
  • Bush picnics and sundowners, planned in advance
Families & leisure

Not just a venue. A weekend together.

Birthdays, anniversaries, reunions or simply your favourite people in one place. Share long meals and evenings around the fire, explore the farm by day and make the occasion your own in a private, intimate setting.

  • Lodge accommodation for up to 16 overnight guests
  • Indoor, gas and wood braais, plus a pizza oven
  • Quad biking, clay pigeon shooting and game drives
  • A cook on request, so the host gets to celebrate too
Groups & celebrations

Beyond the boardroom.

Fresh surroundings, new perspectives, stronger connections. Bring a small team for strategy sessions and planning days, then step outside for shared experiences that build the kind of trust a boardroom can't.

  • A meeting room for small team sessions
  • Spacious dining and lounge areas for working meals
  • Supervised shooting, game drives and quad biking
  • Seven bedrooms and an open loft
Groups & meetings

The hunt is only part of the experience.

Responsible hunting guided by a Professional Hunter (PH) on 800 hectares of private bushveld, with a comfortable lodge to come back to. Ideal for first-time hunters, local groups and corporate outings, and for families who want non-hunters to enjoy the farm too.

  • Underground shooting range and firearm safety guidance
  • Hunting quad bikes and a game vehicle
  • Hunting options confirmed with Marius for your group
  • Butchery and slaughter room for meat processing

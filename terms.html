A booking is confirmed only when we confirm it to you in writing. Rates, deposits, payment, cancellation and any other booking conditions are set out in your quote and booking confirmation, and form part of your agreement with us.

Your stay

The number of overnight guests and the room allocation are confirmed when you book. Guests are asked to follow the farm rules and the reasonable instructions of our staff, and to take care around the swimming pool, the dams and wildlife. Children must be supervised by a responsible adult at all times.

Activities

All activities are offered by prior arrangement and depend on availability, weather and safety conditions. Some carry additional charges or need special equipment, and some have age restrictions. We may decline, change or stop an activity at any time for safety reasons.

Shooting and hunting take place only under supervision and in line with all applicable South African laws, permits and licensing requirements. Guests must follow the safety instructions of the range supervisor or Professional Hunter, whose decisions on safety are final. No firearm handling or shooting is allowed after consuming alcohol. Available species, rates, permits, guiding and equipment are confirmed with each hunting enquiry.
